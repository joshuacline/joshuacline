You’re traveling through another dimension, a dimension not only of sight and sound but of envy, fragility, and fakery; a journey into a disastrous land of those whose destinies are in the hands of but a few seething individuals. Notably the only place you'll find a ninety-nine to one hater ratio, from the windows to the walls. Next stop … the Twilight Zone

***Meet The Developer Killers.***

***Everything's a mirage, get out while you still can.***

***What if I told you:***

Your priceless go-to app made by some random developer (both of which you've never heard of before) are already dead. Name, product, career, figuratively, reputationally, and in some cases literally.

You can kiss anyone you knew goodbye. These people have been doing this for who knows how long, and via playbook. Anyone in your vicinity will be dazzled or frightened (or both) by the display of magical internet hackery-pokery, then recruited, threatened, or simply left with a bad feeling associated with you, the intended victim. Weaponized surroundings, online and offline.
